## About the Collection
This collection contains recent photographs of both urban and natural sites around Minnesota's Twin Cities metropolitan area. The purpose of these images is to provide a snapshot of select locations as they appear in 2026 for comparison to past and future appearances. The collection is intended for use by past, present, and future residents of the Twin Cities as well as anyone else looking for information that may be gleaned from the photographs included.
